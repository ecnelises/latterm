Latterm 26.10.1 — October 4, 2026

Changes since Latterm 26.9.2.

New Features:
- Inspect a profile’s values and their sources.
  In Settings > Profiles, choose Other Actions >
  Inspect Profile Settings to compare defaults
  and restore individual settings.
- Find Settings in Open Quickly. Type /, to
  search only settings and open the right page.

Improvements:
- Read Settings sections in one scrolling page,
  with profile selection beside the editor.
- Keep controls and list actions reachable when
  resizing Settings, including smaller windows.
- Expand the Snippet editor, use monospaced
  text, and save with Command-Return.
- Add guidance to empty Action and Snippet lists
  and searches with no matching results.
- Complete Chinese Settings labels and help,
  including pointer bindings and Snippet help.

Bug Fixes:
- Preserve filtered Snippet lists after editing
  a matching snippet.
- Honor the chosen window, tab, or split option
  when opening files from other applications.
